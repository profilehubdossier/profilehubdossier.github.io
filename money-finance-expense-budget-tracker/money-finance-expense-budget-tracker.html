Privacy? Privacy! And again, Privacy! Privacy is the main concern—how does Profile Hub Dossier manage it?

Profile Hub Dossier is a local-first app. All client profiles, item details, and business information are saved securely on your local device.

  • Profile Hub Dossier does NOT collect your name, location, or phone number.
  • Profile Hub Dossier does NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.
  • Profile Hub Dossier does NOT access your camera, microphone, or photo library without your explicit permission requested through your device's operating system.
  • Profile Hub Dossier does NOT use our own analytics SDKs to track your behavior. Subscription providers may collect limited interaction data (like paywall views) solely to manage services, which is NOT linked to your identity for advertising.
  • More questions? please contact: profilehubdossier@gmail.com
