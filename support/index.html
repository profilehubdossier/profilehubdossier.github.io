Initial Step: Set Up Contacts

The app requires a Contact profile to function. Tap the Person Icon in the bottom right of the home page to open the menu:

  • Access Contacts: Tap the Contact Icon within that sub-menu to open your contact options (Add, Edit, Import, Manage).
  • Manual Entry: Tap Add Contact to enter details manually.
  • Import: Tap Import to securely pull existing contacts directly from your phone's local contact list.
Tip: In the Add or Edit screens, tap the (i) Info Icon in the bottom right to see which fields are required to enable the Save button.

General Workflow

  1. Tap the +New button at the bottom of the home page.
  2. Select an Action (Add, Edit, Report, or Manage) and a Profile (Moneyflow, Cosmetic, or Edu).
  3. Select your Contact and tap the Date → button.

Single Day Vs Date Range

  • Add: Pick a single date from the calendar. This date can represent the purchase day, the record day, or simply the current input date.
  • Edit/Manage/Report: Pick a date range. The system will use this range to find and display all records that fall within it. Ensure the date range you select covers the original purchase or input date of the record you want to manage.
  • The Selection Page: If there is more than one result, you will be prompted to tap and select the specific entry in the selection page to proceed.

Education Profile

This profile utilizes two distinct types of dates:

  • The Single Date (Add Action): This is the date you record the information into the app.
  • Searching: When searching for an Education record, the date range queries the class times (Date 1 and Date 2), NOT the single input date.
  • Date 1 and Date 2: These represent the class start date/time and end date/time. Because these fields define a single class section, you must input the exact same calendar day for both Date 1 and Date 2.
  • Export to Calendar (3-Dot Menu): After inputting Date 1 or Date 2, a 3-dot icon will appear. Tap this icon to export the class schedule directly to your device's native calendar. If a class lasts for multiple days, you can easily extend or adjust the dates within your local system calendar after exporting.

Export / Import

Protecting and transferring your data is simple. You have two backup options:

1. Full Backup

  • Export: Select "Full Backup" to save all data inside PHD. Create a password to secure the file (for example, a password like y123456Y) and tap Export. Pick a location on your device to save it. The file name will be automatically generated based on the exact date and time. For example, a backup created on August 28, 2026, at 9:28:32 AM will be named 08282026092832.phd.
  • Import: To restore a full backup, tap "Select File" and choose your .phd file. Enter the password you created during the export to import the data.

2. Cosmetic

  • Export: First, pick a contact from the "From" drop-down list, then select the style name from the style drop-down. Create a password (for example, y654321Y) and tap Export. The file name format is identical to a full backup, but the extension will be .phdcos (e.g., 03182026083823.phdcos).
  • Import: Ensure you have tapped "Cosmetic" first. Tap Import, pick your .phdcos file, and then select the contact you want to apply this style to from the "To" drop-down list. Enter your password to complete the import.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Our Core Commitment: Profile Hub Dossier is built on a local-first philosophy. Profile data and personal records you create are designed to be stored locally on your device. The PHD team itself does not upload, sell, or share this local data. We rely on trusted third-party services (like Firebase and RevenueCat) for core operations, which handle data independently under their own privacy policies (see Section 4).

1. Who We Are

Profile Hub Dossier ("PHD", "we", "our") is an independent app development team. You can reach us at ProfileHubDossier@gmail.com for any privacy-related questions.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate your account and the app's features:

  • Email Address: Your email is used to ID your account and link it to your subscription and purchase history. It allows our team to verify your account status, restore access if needed, and provide support. We do not use your email for marketing or spam.
  • Purchase & Subscription Status: Managed through RevenueCat to validate your subscription and restore purchases. No full payment or credit card details are stored by our team.
Important: Profile content and personal records you enter into PHD are stored locally on your device. The PHD team does not see, access, or retrieve this local data.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your name, location, or phone number.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.
  • We do NOT access your camera, microphone, or photo library without your explicit permission requested through your device's operating system.
  • We do NOT use our own analytics SDKs to track your behavior. Subscription providers may collect limited interaction data (like paywall views) solely to manage services, which is not linked to your identity for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party services for app functionality. While we do not code our app to share your local data with them, these services may collect certain device or diagnostic data as part of their standard operation. The PHD team does not control these third parties, and their practices are governed by their own policies:

  • Google Firebase — Used to securely store your account credentials (email) for login and account recovery. View Firebase Privacy Policy →
  • RevenueCat — Used to manage subscriptions and restore purchases. They securely process your email and anonymized device identifiers to maintain your subscription. View RevenueCat Privacy Policy →

5. Contact Permissions (Optional)

With your explicit permission, PHD can import contacts from your phone's local address book to help you set up profiles. This is entirely optional. Any contact data pulled into the app is processed locally, and the PHD team does not transmit, upload, or share this data with our servers or third parties.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your account seriously:

  • Account credentials are handled by Google Firebase using industry-standard encryption.
  • Profile data lives on your local device, safeguarded by your device's native security (Face ID, passcode, etc.).
  • Disclaimer: While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security against unauthorized access or device compromise.

7. Your Rights

You are in control of your data:

  • Delete your account: Contact us at ProfileHubDossier@gmail.com and we will delete your account data (email and UID) from our active database.
  • Delete your local data: Use the in-app Reset button to wipe local data instantly, or simply uninstall the app. Either action permanently removes the profile data stored on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

PHD is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove it.
