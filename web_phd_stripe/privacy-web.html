Privacy Policy

Profile Hub Dossier web version. Last updated: October 2026.

In short: your records stay in a file on your own device. PHD does not keep a copy of your file on its servers. We keep only your email address and your subscription status so we can sign you in and manage your plan. Card payments are handled by Stripe.

1. Who we are

Profile Hub Dossier ("PHD", "we", "our") is an independent app made by Fryinocean. This policy covers the PHD web version at this site. You can reach us at profilehubdossier@gmail.com with any privacy question.

2. What we collect

  • Email address. Used to send you a sign-in link, to identify your account, to link it to your subscription, and to give you support. We do not use it for marketing or spam.
  • Subscription and billing status. Your plan, your trial and billing dates, and a reference to your customer record at Stripe, so the site knows what you can use.
  • Basic technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider may process standard data such as your IP address and browser type in server logs.

We do not collect your name, your address, your location, or your phone number.

3. What stays on your device

  • The records you create (cosmetic, spending, education and contact details) live in a file you download and keep. When you work, your browser opens that file in its own memory. PHD does not upload your file to our servers.
  • If you import contacts (from a contact file or your device's contact picker), they are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • If you close the page without downloading, unsaved changes are lost, and we cannot recover them, because we do not hold your file.
  • The file is a standard database file that PHD does not encrypt. Anyone who can open the file on your device or in your cloud storage can read it. Please protect it with your device security or encrypted storage.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to anyone.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers in the web app.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or location.

5. Third-party services

We rely on trusted services. They handle some data under their own policies, which we do not control:

  • Google Firebase provides sign-in by email link, stores your account and subscription status, and hosts the PHD web app. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts this information site (home, terms and privacy pages) and may process standard data such as your IP address in its server logs. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Stripe processes payments and subscriptions. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach PHD. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Stripe processes payments and subscriptions. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach PHD. Stripe Privacy Policy

    • 6. Browser storage

    To keep you signed in, and to finish the email sign-in link, the site stores a sign-in session and your email in your browser's storage on your device. You will stay signed in until the session expires, you sign out, or you clear your browser data. Clearing it only signs you out. It does not delete your file.

    7. Keeping and deleting data

    • We keep your email and subscription records while your account is active.
    • To delete your account data, email us. We will delete your email and account record from our database, and we will help you cancel any active subscription first.
    • Stripe may keep payment records as required by law or for accounting.
    • Your own file is on your device. Delete it yourself whenever you like.

    8. Security

    Sign-in and account data are handled by Google Firebase using industry-standard protection, and payments by Stripe. No method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    9. Children

    PHD is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

    10. The iPhone app

    The PHD iPhone app is a separate product with its own policy: iPhone app Privacy Policy.

    11. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site means you accept the update.

    12. Contact

    Questions, deletion requests or concerns: profilehubdossier@gmail.com
