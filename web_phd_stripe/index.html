Questions

What do I get with the free trial?

14 days of the Base plan: Add, Edit, Summary and Manage across the profiles. A card is required, you are not charged during the trial, and the first charge happens when it ends unless you cancel.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you already paid for. There is no refund for a month already paid. For help, email profilehubdossier@gmail.com.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Your records stay in your own file on your own device, since PHD does not hold them. After a trial or subscription ends, you can still open your file and use the Summary view.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes. The Profile Hub Dossier iPhone app is available on the App Store. It is a separate product with its own subscription.
